Privacy Policy

Simple Chess (com.tadeupassos.simplechess) · Effective October 19, 2026

Simple Chess is a chess board for two people playing on one device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. This policy explains what that means in detail.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

To keep your preferences and let you resume an interrupted game, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your settings (board colors, orientation, sound, vibration and similar options);
  • the game in progress and its clock times.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Game analysis

The post-game analysis uses the Stockfish chess engine, which runs entirely on your device. No positions or games are sent anywhere.

Permissions and device features

  • Internet: included by the app framework. Simple Chess works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. Links in the Settings page (to license and source code pages) open in your browser only if you tap them.
  • Vibration: a short vibration when a move is made, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Keep screen on: the screen stays awake during a game so it doesn't turn off while the clock is running.
  • Clipboard: the app writes to the clipboard only when you choose to copy a game. It never reads your clipboard.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
